The Story

Over two decades shaping luxury fashion, with an eye for quality and a heart for purpose.

Lebo Davies is a visionary entrepreneur whose passion for fashion and design has shaped the ethos of OnlyBrands. With over two decades of experience in the industry, she has successfully navigated the dynamic landscape of luxury fashion, emerging as a prominent figure known for her innovative approach and keen eye for quality.

Lebo’s journey began in Johannesburg, where her love for contemporary design and rich cultural heritage inspired her to create pieces that resonate with elegance and sophistication. As the driving force behind OnlyBrands, she has cultivated a brand that not only offers exceptional products but also embodies the values of inclusivity and sustainability.

Under her leadership, OnlyBrands has become synonymous with curated luxury at accessible prices, attracting a discerning clientele that appreciates both style and substance. Lebo’s commitment to ethical sourcing and environmental responsibility reflects her belief that fashion can be both beautiful and conscious.
